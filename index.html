How to install

  1. Tap Download, then open the file when it finishes.
  2. If Android asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser (or WhatsApp, or Files).
  3. If Play Protect warns you, tap More details → Install anyway. RideWise is not in the Play Store, so Google has not seen it.
  4. Open RideWise and sign up with your email.

Updates

When a new version is out, RideWise tells you on its Dashboard. Tap Update now, then Install. Your vehicles, records and photos stay.

The first time, Android asks you to allow RideWise to install updates. That is only for its own updates.

Your data

  • Your records are kept in your own account, and only you can see them.
  • Photos of documents, receipts and inspections stay on your phone.
  • Your location is used only when you tap My location, on the phone, and is never stored.
  • Saved places (Home, Work) stay on your phone.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it from Profile.
